# Keep

Keep is open-source (Apache-2.0) storage and distribution of secrets for humans and AI agents.
It works standalone and plugs into [Mesh](https://github.com/entire-vc/evc-mesh).

> Status: early development. There is no release yet, and interfaces will change.

## Goals

- Store secrets once and hand them out to the people and agents that need them.
- Keep secret values out of logs, transcripts and chat.
- Run on its own, or as a Mesh integration.
