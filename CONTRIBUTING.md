# Contributing to Keep

Keep is at an early stage. This guide will grow as the project takes shape.

## Ground rules

- `main` is protected: every change lands through a merge request, and no one pushes to it directly.
- Keep merge requests small and focused, and describe what changed and how you tested it.
- Never commit secrets, including in tests, fixtures or examples. Use obviously fake values.
